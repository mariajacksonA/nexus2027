About NEXUS 2027

A platform for sustainable innovation

NEXUS 2027 is an international conference focused on emerging research, technological innovation and interdisciplinary approaches in sustainable materials, energy and environmental systems.

The conference brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators and students to exchange knowledge, present research outcomes and explore collaborative opportunities addressing sustainability challenges.

NEXUS represents the connection between scientific knowledge, engineering innovation and sustainable development.

Materials × Energy × Environment

Three interconnected areas form the scientific foundation of NEXUS 2027, encouraging research exchange and collaboration across these fields.

About the Institution

SRM TRP Engineering College is headed by Dr. R. Shivakumar, M.D., Ph.D., Chairman, Ramapuram and Trichy Campuses. The institution continues the legacy of excellence in education associated with the SRM Group of Institutions. It is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

The institution offers eight undergraduate programmes, three postgraduate programmes and three Ph.D. programmes. It provides basic and advanced amenities, modern teaching methodologies, well-equipped laboratories, and highly qualified and experienced faculty. A conducive learning environment supported by state-of-the-art resources and a dedicated Training and Placement Cell supports students’ academic and career development.

The institution has received a NAAC A+ accreditation and Green Gown Awards recognition, and has signed the SDG Accord.

Mechanical Engineering

The Department of Mechanical Engineering was established in 2010 and offers B.E. Mechanical Engineering, M.E. Thermal Engineering and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering. The B.E. Mechanical Engineering programme has NBA accreditation since 2021.

The department’s research and project areas include tool design, composite materials, cryogenic processes, machining simulation, total quality control, welding technology, corrosion, fuel cells and mobility engineering. The department also has an Anna University-approved research centre and research facilities supporting advanced academic and research activities.
